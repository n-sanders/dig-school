# Image Credits — Dig School Lesson Images

> **Use note:** this course is built for personal, non-commercial homeschool
> education. Open-licensed images (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) are
> preferred and used wherever they fit — see each entry below. Where no open
> image fits, a high-quality image from a museum, university, government, or
> educational site may be used instead, with full credit (title, creator or
> institution, source URL, and rights status) recorded here and in the
> caption. All images are stored locally in `public/images/lessons/` — the
> site never hotlinks. The watercolor illustrations below are original
> AI-generated illustrations made for this project, for personal homeschool
> use.

Lesson bodies currently use **69 images**: **28 sourced photographs**,
**10 created SVG diagrams**, and **30 original watercolor illustrations**.
Every image has descriptive alt text and an italic caption carrying its
credit. SVG files retired from lessons remain on disk (and regenerable with
`scripts/make-lesson-images.py`) for the future illustration pass.

- **Location:** `public/images/lessons/` (`*.svg`, `photo-*.jpg`, and the
  watercolor `*.jpg` sets), referenced from lesson bodies as
  `images/lessons/<file>` (the project's local-asset format).

## Sourced photographs (28)

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons with `scripts/download-sourced-images.py`,
which re-checks each file's live license metadata — only public domain, CC0,
CC BY, and CC BY-SA files were accepted. Every photo was visually inspected
before use; rejected candidates (a BLM lab-prep photo mis-titled as field
jackets, an NPS "bake off" cookie photo, and a BLM rock-face photo with no
visible fossil) are not used. Three formerly used photos were later replaced
by better-fitting images and removed: the Green River fish (burial
watercolors), the NPS museum cabinets (catalog watercolor), and the USDA
T. rex tooth (teeth watercolors).
